We're thrilled to announce one of our most significant updates yet! This August release transforms Captain VFR into a truly international flight planning tool while introducing powerful new features that make flight planning faster and more intuitive than ever before.

## 🌍 Complete Multilingual Support

### Seven Languages, One Seamless Experience

Captain VFR now speaks your language! We've added comprehensive support for **seven languages**:

- **English** - Complete and enhanced
- **German (Deutsch)** - Professional aviation terminology
- **French (Français)** - Fully localized interface
- **Spanish (Español)** - Comprehensive translation
- **Italian (Italiano)** - Complete aviation context
- **Czech (Čeština)** - Native language support
- **Slovak (Slovenčina)** - Full localization

### Professional Aviation Standards

Every translation maintains **professional aviation terminology** while ensuring clarity and consistency across all languages. Whether you're planning a flight in Prague, Barcelona, or Rome, Captain VFR speaks your language with proper aviation context.

### Instant Language Switching

- **No app restart required** - Switch languages instantly
- **Preference persistence** - Your language choice is saved across sessions
- **Regional formatting** - Dates, numbers, and measurements adapt to your locale
- **Complete interface coverage** - Every button, menu, and dialog is translated

## 🔍 Smart Search History

### Never Lose Your Frequently Used Locations

The new **Search History** feature remembers your last **10 selected airports and navaids**, making return visits lightning-fast:

- **Instant access** to your most-used destinations
- **Persistent across sessions** - Your history survives app restarts
- **Smart organization** - Most recent selections appear first
- **One-tap selection** - Quick access to frequent locations
- **Clear history option** - Start fresh whenever needed

This feature is perfect for pilots with regular routes, flight instructors working from specific airports, or anyone who frequently returns to the same locations.

## 📱 Unified Menu Panel

### Redesigned for Better Usability

We've completely reimagined the menu system with a **single sliding panel** that consolidates all controls:

### Perfect for Landscape Mode
- **Optimized for iPhone landscape** orientation
- **Responsive design** that adapts to any screen size
- **Single slide-out panel** from the right side
- **Compact layout** with all essential controls

### Everything in One Place
- Flight planning tools
- Map layer controls
- Settings and preferences
- Search functionality
- User profile and sync options

The new design eliminates the need to hunt through multiple panels, especially beneficial during flight when quick access to controls is essential.

## ⏱️ Auto-Center Timer Display

### Visual Feedback for Map Tracking

When using auto-center mode, you now see exactly when the map will return to tracking your position:

- **Countdown timer** showing "Auto in: MM:SS"
- **Clear visual feedback** for tracking behavior
- **Helps prevent confusion** when manually panning the map
- **Smooth transition** back to tracking mode

This feature eliminates guesswork about when the map will auto-center, giving you confidence when temporarily exploring areas off your current position.
