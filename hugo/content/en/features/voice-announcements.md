# Voice Announcements

Keep your eyes on the sky and hands on the controls with intelligent voice announcements. CaptainVFR speaks to you about important events, letting you focus on flying while staying informed.

## Overview

Voice announcements provide hands-free audio alerts for critical flight events. The system uses text-to-speech technology to deliver clear, timely information without requiring you to look at your device.

## Key Features

### Waypoint Announcements

#### Proximity Alerts
- **"Approaching [Waypoint Name]"**
  - Triggered at 1 nautical mile
  - Helps with navigation awareness
  - Prepares you for next leg

#### Waypoint Reached
- **"[Waypoint Name] reached"**
  - Confirms waypoint passage
  - Automatic progress tracking
  - No need to check screen

### Airspace Warnings

#### Proximity Warnings
- **"Approaching [Airspace Name]"**
  - Triggered at 5 nautical miles
  - Gives time to prepare
  - Includes airspace type (Class B, C, D, etc.)

#### Entry Alerts
- **"Entering [Airspace Name]"**
  - Confirms airspace entry
  - Reminds you of requirements
  - Helps maintain compliance

### Terrain Clearance Warnings

#### Warning Levels
Based on clearance above terrain:

**Caution (1000-2000 ft clearance)**
- **"Terrain"**
- Single alert
- Awareness level

**Warning (500-1000 ft clearance)**
- **"Terrain, terrain"**
- Repeated twice
- Increased urgency

**Critical (<500 ft clearance)**
- **"PULL UP, PULL UP"**
- Loud and urgent
- Immediate action required

## Customization

### Voice Settings

#### Volume Control
- Adjustable from 0% to 100%
- Independent of device volume
- Slider control in settings
- Real-time preview

#### Speech Rate
- Adjustable from 10% to 100%
- Slower for better clarity
- Faster for experienced pilots
- Find your comfortable pace

#### Pitch
- Adjustable from 0.5 to 2.0
- Lower pitch for calm alerts
- Higher pitch for urgency
- Customize to preference

### Enable/Disable
- Master on/off toggle
- Quick access in settings
- Persists across flights
- No announcements when disabled

## Smart Behavior

### Duplicate Prevention
- Won't repeat same announcement
- Tracks announced waypoints
- Prevents announcement spam
- Resets when tracking stops

### Cooldown Periods
- Terrain warnings: 10 seconds
- Prevents alert fatigue
- Escalates if situation worsens
- Smart timing

### Distance Thresholds
- **Waypoints**: 1 nautical mile (1852m)
- **Airspaces**: 5 nautical miles (9260m)
- **Terrain**: Based on clearance
- Optimized for reaction time
