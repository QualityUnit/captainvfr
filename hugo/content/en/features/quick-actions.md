# Quick Action Bar

Access the most important features instantly with the Quick Action Bar. Five essential actions are always available at the bottom center of your screen, putting critical functionality at your fingertips.

## Location & Design

### Strategic Positioning
- **Bottom Center**: Easy thumb reach
- **Always Visible**: Never hidden
- **Compact Design**: Doesn't obstruct map
- **Color-Coded**: Quick visual identification

### Responsive Layout
- Adapts to screen size
- Works in portrait and landscape
- Touch-optimized buttons
- Haptic feedback (when available)

## The Five Essential Actions

### 1. 📍 Center Map
**Blue Button - Left Position**

- **Function**: Centers map on your current position
- **Use Case**: Quick reorientation after panning
- **Visual**: Location pin icon
- **Shortcut**: Double-tap map (alternative)

**When to Use:**
- After exploring the map
- Lost your position
- Need quick reorientation
- Starting navigation

### 2. ✈️ Flight Plan
**Green Button - Second Position**

- **Function**: Opens flight planning panel
- **Use Case**: Create, edit, or view flight plans
- **Visual**: Airplane icon
- **State Indicator**: Highlights when flight plan active

**Features:**
- Create new flight plan
- Edit existing plan
- View route on map
- Calculate distance and time
- Add/remove waypoints

**When to Use:**
- Planning a flight
- Modifying route
- Checking progress
- Adding waypoints

### 3. 🌤️ Weather
**Yellow Button - Center Position**

- **Function**: Toggles weather overlay
- **Use Case**: View METAR/TAF data on map
- **Visual**: Cloud/sun icon
- **State Indicator**: Highlights when weather visible

**Weather Display:**
- Airport weather markers
- Color-coded by flight category:
  - 🟢 Green: VFR
  - 🟡 Yellow: MVFR
  - 🔴 Red: IFR
  - ⚫ Black: LIFR
- METAR text on tap
- TAF forecasts
- Weather age indicator

**When to Use:**
- Pre-flight planning
- En-route weather checks
- Destination weather
- Alternate selection

### 4. 🛡️ Airspaces
**Purple Button - Fourth Position**

- **Function**: Toggles airspace overlay
- **Use Case**: View controlled airspace boundaries
- **Visual**: Shield icon
- **State Indicator**: Highlights when airspaces visible

**Airspace Display:**
- Class B, C, D airspace
- Special use airspace
- Temporary flight restrictions
- Military operations areas
- Altitude-based filtering
- Frequency information

**When to Use:**
- Route planning
- Avoiding controlled airspace
- Finding frequencies
- Checking restrictions

### 5. 🚨 Emergency
**Red Button - Right Position**

- **Function**: Opens emergency panel
- **Use Case**: Instant access to emergency information
- **Visual**: Warning icon
- **Priority**: Always accessible

**Emergency Panel Includes:**
- 121.5 MHz emergency frequency
- Current position
- Nearest airports
- Emergency checklist
- Quick reference procedures

**When to Use:**
- Emergency situations
- Practice drills
- Quick reference
- Position reporting

## Usage Patterns

### Pre-Flight
1. **Weather**: Check conditions along route
2. **Airspaces**: Review airspace to avoid
3. **Flight Plan**: Create or load flight plan
4. **Center Map**: Verify starting position

### In-Flight
1. **Center Map**: Maintain situational awareness
2. **Weather**: Monitor changing conditions
3. **Airspaces**: Stay clear of restricted areas
4. **Flight Plan**: Track progress

### Emergency
1. **Emergency**: Immediate access to critical info
2. **Center Map**: Confirm position
3. **Weather**: Check nearest good weather
4. **Flight Plan**: Divert if needed
