# Flight Tracking & Logging

Every flight tells a story—where you went, how you got there, what conditions you encountered, and how you performed. CaptainVFR's flight tracking system records that story automatically, creating detailed GPS tracks, performance data, and flight logs that help you analyze your flying, maintain accurate records, and continuously improve your skills.

From the moment you start moving until you shut down, CaptainVFR records your position, altitude, speed, and heading with precision GPS tracking. The result is a complete record of every flight—a breadcrumb trail showing your exact path, altitude profiles revealing your climb and descent performance, and comprehensive statistics documenting your flying. This data flows seamlessly into your digital logbook, transforming raw GPS tracks into official logbook entries with minimal effort.

## Visual Flight Review & Analysis

The real value of flight tracking emerges when you review your flights after landing. CaptainVFR's flight review tools transform raw GPS data into visual, interactive analysis that helps you understand your performance and identify areas for improvement.

The map replay feature lets you watch your flight unfold on the map, with a moving aircraft icon following your exact path. Scrub through the timeline to jump to any point in the flight, or play it back at various speeds. The altitude chart displays your altitude profile graphically, making it easy to see climb performance, altitude maintenance during cruise, and descent profiles. Speed charts show ground speed variations throughout the flight, revealing where you encountered headwinds or tailwinds.

For training flights, the review tools are invaluable. See exactly where you entered the pattern, how your pattern legs looked from above, and where you turned base and final. Analyze steep turns to see if you maintained altitude and completed perfect 360-degree circles. Review cross-country navigation to see how accurately you tracked your planned course. The visual feedback accelerates learning and helps you refine your technique.

## Seamless Logbook Integration

Flight tracking and logbook keeping are naturally connected, and CaptainVFR makes the connection seamless. Every recorded flight can be converted to a logbook entry with a single tap, automatically filling in departure and arrival airports, flight times, route flown, and conditions encountered.

The system uses GPS data to determine departure and arrival airports automatically, identifies day vs. night flight time based on official sunset/sunrise times, calculates cross-country time based on distance flown, and determines dual vs. solo time based on your settings. You add the details that GPS can't capture—pilot in command time, instructor name, maneuvers practiced, and personal notes—and the logbook entry is complete.

For pilots building hours toward ratings or maintaining currency, this integration is transformative. No more reconstructing flights from memory or estimating times. Every flight is documented accurately with precise times, exact routes, and complete details. Your logbook becomes a true record of your flying, not an approximation.

## Pattern Work Analysis

For pilots practicing takeoffs and landings, CaptainVFR's pattern work analysis provides detailed feedback on your pattern flying. The system automatically detects touch-and-goes and stop-and-goes, identifies pattern legs (crosswind, downwind, base, final), and analyzes your pattern geometry.

See if your downwind leg was parallel to the runway, whether you turned base at the right point, and how your final approach looked. The system measures pattern altitude consistency, identifies altitude deviations, and shows where you were high or low. For student pilots and pilots working on proficiency, this objective feedback is invaluable for improving pattern work.

## Data Management & Privacy

Your flight data is yours, and CaptainVFR gives you complete control. All flights are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to the cloud for backup and cross-device access. You control what syncs and what stays local. Delete any flight permanently if desired, or archive old flights to free up space while maintaining records.

Export flights in standard formats—GPX for use in other aviation apps, KML for Google Earth, CSV for spreadsheet analysis, or PDF for printable flight reports. The export includes all recorded data—position, altitude, speed, and statistics. This data portability ensures you're never locked into a single platform and can use your flight data however you choose.

For privacy, you can enable anonymous mode that records flights without uploading to cloud services, obscure exact departure and arrival locations if desired, and control who can see your flights and flight statistics. Your flying is your business, and CaptainVFR respects that.
