## Intelligent Conflict Resolution

Life happens. Sometimes you modify a flight plan on your tablet while your phone is in airplane mode, then make different changes on your phone when you land. CaptainVFR's intelligent conflict resolution system detects these situations and handles them gracefully, using smart merge algorithms to combine changes when possible and presenting clear options when human judgment is needed. You never lose data, and you're always in control.

Our synchronization system tracks every change with precise timestamps and device identifiers, allowing it to understand the sequence of events even when devices are offline for extended periods. When conflicts arise, the system analyzes the changes and either merges them automatically (when changes affect different parts of the data) or presents you with a clear comparison view to choose which version to keep. It's sophisticated technology that works invisibly, only asking for your input when truly necessary.

## Security & Privacy First

Your aviation data is sensitive and personal. CaptainVFR treats it with the security it deserves, using military-grade encryption for all data in transit and at rest, secure authentication with optional two-factor authentication, granular privacy controls over what syncs and what stays local, and complete data ownership with easy export options. Your data is yours, always encrypted, always under your control.

Every synchronization session uses TLS 1.3 encryption, the same technology that protects your banking transactions. Data stored in the cloud is encrypted with AES-256, and encryption keys are derived from your password using industry-standard key derivation functions. Even CaptainVFR's servers can't decrypt your data without your password. You can enable two-factor authentication for an additional security layer, and you can export all your data at any time in standard formats.

## Seamless Device Transitions

Switching devices should be effortless. When you get a new phone or tablet, CaptainVFR makes the transition seamless. Sign in with your account, and within minutes, all your flight plans, aircraft profiles, documents, and settings are synchronized to your new device. No manual transfers, no data loss, no configuration hassles. Your new device is ready to fly as soon as the sync completes.

For offline transitions, CaptainVFR supports local device-to-device transfer via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct, perfect for situations where you need to move data without internet access. The system handles everything automatically, transferring your complete aviation database while maintaining data integrity and encryption throughout the process.
